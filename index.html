Map of Outdoor Citrus

Lemons, limes, oranges and grapefruit are not only surviving, but thriving through winters outdoors in London and beyond. Nobody knows how many there are, but we are adding as many as we can find to this map.

Spotted one in a park, a front garden or your own back garden? Put it on the map.
